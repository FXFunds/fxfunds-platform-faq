## Important Notes

- A company's public filing does not automatically establish that every service advertised by its platform is licensed or regulated.
- An SEC Form D filing is not a brokerage license, an investment approval, or an endorsement by the SEC.
- Company descriptions of security features, payment options, audits, and customer support should not be interpreted as independent verification of their effectiveness or availability.
- Repository content is informational and should not be treated as financial or investment advice.
- The date in the answer register indicates the documentation review date. It does not necessarily mean every underlying statement was independently reverified on that date.
- Repository ownership and official company authorization should be confirmed before describing this repository as an official FXFunds resource.

## How to Update an Answer

1. Identify the FAQ that needs to be updated.
2. Locate the appropriate official company document, regulatory record, technical document, or repository file.
3. Update the answer, status, supporting source, and review date in `fxfunds_platform_faq/faq.json`.
4. Update this `SOURCES.md` file to reflect the revised answer and its supporting material.
5. Regenerate or update `README.md`, the relevant topic pages, `schema.json`, and `dataset/fxfunds_platform_faq.csv` where applicable.
6. Run `pytest` to check consistency across the repository.
7. Record the changes in `CHANGELOG.md`.
